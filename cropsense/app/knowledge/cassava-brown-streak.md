# Cassava Brown Streak Disease

Crop: cassava  
Class id: Cassava___brown_streak_disease

## What it is

Cassava brown streak disease is a virus problem. Leaves may show yellow patches, especially between veins on older leaves. Storage roots can have brown dry rot inside, which makes them unsellable. Whiteflies and infected cuttings spread it. Root symptoms may appear even when leaves look only mildly sick.

## Treatment options

- There is no pesticide that removes the virus from an infected plant.
- Uproot plants with clear leaf symptoms and do not eat or sell roots that have brown dry streaks inside.
- Replace the whole seed lot if many plants show symptoms.

## Prevention

- Use clean, official cuttings of varieties recommended against brown streak in your zone, if available.
- Never save cuttings from a field that had rotten brown-streaked roots.
- Report severe outbreaks to your extension office so the community can change seed together.
- Control is through clean planting material, not through a spray programme invented at home.
