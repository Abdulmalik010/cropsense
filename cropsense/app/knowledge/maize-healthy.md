# Maize — healthy leaf

Crop: maize  
Class id: Maize___healthy

## What it is

This leaf looks healthy: even colour, no long cigar lesions, no rusty pustules, and no gray rectangular spots between veins.

## Treatment options

- No disease spray is needed for this leaf.

## Prevention

- Walk the field every week in the rains and check lower and upper leaves.
- Photograph any new spots on other plants.
- Keep recommended spacing and remove old maize residue after harvest.
