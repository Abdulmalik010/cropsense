# Tomato — healthy leaf

Crop: tomato  
Class id: Tomato___healthy

## What it is

The leaf in the photo looks like a healthy tomato leaf: even green colour, no target spots, no water-soaked patches, and no strong upward yellow curl. Healthy does not guarantee the whole field is free of pests.

## Treatment options

- No disease treatment is needed for this leaf.
- Continue normal care: water the soil, not the leaves, and watch weekly for new spots.

## Prevention

- Keep scouting during humid weather.
- Remove yellowing lower leaves that touch the soil.
- Use clean stakes and avoid walking through wet plants.
- If other plants in the same bed look sick, photograph those leaves instead.
