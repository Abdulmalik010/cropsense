# Maize Gray Leaf Spot

Crop: maize  
Class id: Maize___Gray_leaf_spot

## What it is

Gray leaf spot is a fungal disease. Lesions are rectangular, pale brown to gray, and strictly limited by leaf veins, looking like matchsticks on the leaf. It is worse where maize is planted every year, residues remain on the surface, and humidity is high.

## Treatment options

- Do not apply an unnamed chemical from memory.
- In a severe outbreak on a commercial field, an extension officer may discuss a registered fungicide. Follow only labelled rates if a product is recommended.
- Prioritise residue burial and a different variety next season.

## Prevention

- Rotate away from maize for a season if you can.
- Incorporate or remove last season’s maize trash.
- Use tolerant hybrids or OPVs recommended in your state, if sold.
- Avoid very dense planting that keeps leaves wet for long periods.
