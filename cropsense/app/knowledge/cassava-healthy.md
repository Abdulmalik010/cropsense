# Cassava — healthy leaf

Crop: cassava  
Class id: Cassava___healthy

## What it is

This cassava leaf looks healthy: no mosaic mottling, no angular water-soaked blight spots, and no strong yellowing between veins on older leaves.

## Treatment options

- No disease treatment is needed for this leaf.

## Prevention

- Still inspect roots at harvest for brown dry rot (brown streak can hide).
- Take next season’s cuttings only from vigorous, symptom-free plants.
- Keep whitefly-host weeds down at the field edge.
