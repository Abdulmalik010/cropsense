# Cassava Mosaic Disease

Crop: cassava  
Class id: Cassava___mosaic_disease

## What it is

Cassava mosaic disease is caused by viruses. Leaves show yellow-green mosaic, distortion, and reduced size. Severely affected plants are stunted and give few or small roots. Whiteflies spread the virus. Infected cuttings also carry it into a new farm. There is no chemical that cures a virus in the plant.

## Treatment options

- Rogue (uproot and destroy) plants with strong mosaic, especially in the first months after planting, so they do not feed whiteflies.
- Do not take cuttings from mosaic plants, even if the stems look thick.
- Insecticide against whiteflies is not a substitute for clean cuttings. If anyone suggests spraying, only a local extension officer should name a registered product and rate. CropSense does not.

## Prevention

- Plant only mosaic-free cuttings from a trusted source or an official clean-seed scheme (for example IITA / NRCRI / ADP programmes where they exist).
- Prefer officially released mosaic-resistant varieties if they are available in your area.
- Do not mix cuttings from a sick neighbour’s farm into your seed bundle.
- Visit the field often in the first 2–3 months and remove sick plants.
