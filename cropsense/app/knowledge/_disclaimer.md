# Disclaimer

CropSense is an open-source decision-support tool for smallholder farmers.

It is not a replacement for a trained agronomist, plant clinic, or agricultural extension officer.

Do not apply any pesticide, fungicide, or insecticide unless a local extension officer confirms the product is registered for that crop and use in your state, and explains a safe dose and waiting period.

When unsure, take another clear photo and visit a nearby ADP / extension office.
