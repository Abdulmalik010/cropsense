# Pepper Bacterial Spot

Crop: pepper  
Class id: Pepper___Bacterial_spot

## What it is

Bacterial spot makes small, dark, water-soaked leaf spots that may turn brown with a yellow halo. Spots can be angular. Leaves drop early. Fruit may get raised scabby spots. Rain splash, infected seed or seedlings, and working wet plants spread the bacteria. Hot, wet weather favours it.

## Treatment options

- Remove badly spotted leaves and destroy them away from the bed.
- Water the soil, not the foliage.
- Do not harvest or prune when plants are wet.
- A local extension officer may recommend a copper product that is registered for pepper bacterial spot. Only use it if they confirm the product and the label rate. CropSense does not invent a brand, mix, or spray interval.

## Prevention

- Buy clean seed or seedlings; avoid bargains with spotted leaves.
- Rotate pepper and tomato so they do not follow each other in the same bed every year.
- Stake plants and keep mulch on the soil to reduce splash.
- Do not smoke tobacco around seedlings (unrelated viruses can also move on hands; wash hands before handling plants).
