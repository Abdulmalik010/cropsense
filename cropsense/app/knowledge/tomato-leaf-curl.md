# Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus

Crop: tomato  
Class id: Tomato___Tomato_Yellow_Leaf_Curl_Virus

## What it is

Tomato yellow leaf curl is a virus disease. Leaves become small, yellow at the edges, and curl upward. Plants may be stunted and set little fruit. The virus is spread by whiteflies, not by wind or by cutting tools as the main route. You cannot “wash off” a virus with soap once the plant is infected.

## Treatment options

- There is no chemical that kills the virus inside the plant.
- Pull out and destroy badly stunted plants so they do not feed whiteflies.
- Reduce whiteflies with field sanitation and, if available, insect-proof nursery nets.
- Insecticide use against whiteflies must only follow advice from a local extension officer using products registered for tomato in your state. CropSense does not name a product or dose.

## Prevention

- Raise seedlings under fine netting to keep whiteflies off young plants.
- Avoid planting new tomato next to old, infested plots.
- Remove weeds that host whiteflies around the bed.
- Ask your extension office about varieties with some tolerance, if sold locally.
- Do not keep old volunteer tomato plants.
