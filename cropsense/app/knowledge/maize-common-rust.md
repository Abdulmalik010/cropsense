# Maize Common Rust

Crop: maize  
Class id: Maize___Common_rust

## What it is

Common rust makes small, circular to elongated cinnamon-brown pustules on both sides of the maize leaf. Pustules break the surface and leave a rusty powder. Heavy rust dries leaves and can reduce yield, especially on late or susceptible varieties. Cool to moderate temperatures and dew favour the fungus.

## Treatment options

- For most small plots, spraying is not the first step.
- If rust is severe on a seed or sweet-maize crop, ask an extension officer whether a registered fungicide is justified. CropSense does not provide a product name or dose.
- Keep the crop well nourished with recommended fertilizer practice for your area so plants can tolerate some rust.

## Prevention

- Choose locally recommended varieties with rust resistance when possible.
- Plant at the recommended time for your zone so grain fill does not coincide with the worst rust weather.
- Destroy volunteer maize.
- Avoid planting only one very susceptible variety across the whole farm.
